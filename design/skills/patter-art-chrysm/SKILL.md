# patter_art_chrysm

Chrysm is the house style of the BoF Atelier Field Guide. Its common elements are a warm paper ground, a drawn contour and mineral colour. Subjects and compositions can change. The two books have distinct visual languages.

Choose the closest plate and inspect its image before writing the prompt. For a new location, determine where the illustration will be cropped and where the interface puts its text. For an object, decide what the silhouette must communicate at thumbnail size.

| Purpose | Reference | Decisions to carry forward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| III landscape | [Highlands](references/highlands.webp) | Build distance with cool blue washes. Give the foreground animal, plants or stone a warm edge. Use fine ink, gouache and toothy paper. Leave mist or valley to the left and keep the face in the right half. |
| IV landscape | [Sand sea](references/sandsea.webp) | Carry movement through an asymmetric sweep of dry brush. Use celadon, amber sand and aubergine ink with a small cinnabar detail. Give the distant settlement or travellers a believable scale. The painting occupies the left leaf of the Campaign spread. |
| Field specimens | [Flame](references/flame.webp), [Frost](references/frost.webp), [Thunder](references/thunder.webp) | Draw on ivory, with a small contact shadow and room around the silhouette. Use shape as well as hue. Preserve the same scale, light direction and padding across a collection. |

Use the matching [original prompt and adaptation notes](references/prompts.md). Replace the subject, location, light and composition details deliberately. Keep typography, interface controls and labels in the layout. Generate original illustrations for atmosphere. When a player needs to recognise an exact game sprite or place, use a separately verified local reference in the game guide.

When a user asks for an image, use the image generator available for that task with the closest approved plate as a style reference when the tool supports it. For a prompt-only request, deliver the finished prompt. Retain the prompt, reference filenames, generation tool/version when visible, original output and crop as the provenance of a new plate. Record unknown values as unknown. A new candidate can sit beside these five originals.

Review the whole composition, the actual screen crop and a thumbnail. Look for readable silhouettes, a light source that belongs to the scene, paper visible through the pigment and enough quiet space for the surrounding page. Compare both books side by side when the same subject appears in each.
