## Breath of Fire III landscape

> Wide landscape full-bleed editorial watercolour plate, aspect 3:2, for an original Japanese fantasy role-playing travel field guide. Close, magnificent small agile winged dragon perched on a copper-crystal outcrop in a green mountain valley; dragon in middle and RIGHT of frame, long neck, clearly drawn intelligent expressive profile facing left, warm burnished ochre wing membranes outstretched, jade and petrol blue scales, spring grass, wildflowers, rivers and terraces winding into azure distant crags. Morning sun cutting across ridges, strong silhouette, gouache, fine ink and mineral pigment on toothy warm ivory paper. Painterly 1990s illustrated travel atlas production quality, finely observed anatomy, romantic scale. Left third open blue mist/valley, suitable for cropping into a tall specimen view on the right. Entire canvas is painting. No text, diagrams, borders, seals, writing, logos, humans or reference to any existing game characters.

For another landscape, substitute a named subject and terrain. Write a sentence describing how the atmospheric perspective changes from the foreground to the horizon. Choose space for the intended crop.

## Breath of Fire IV landscape

> A full-bleed panoramic ink and mineral pigment painting for an original fantasy desert travel journal, landscape aspect ratio 3:2. Viewed from a hillside, gigantic sinuous sand wyrm like a breaching whale arches through a rolling sea of windswept ochre dunes on the RIGHT. A thin caravan and tiny cliffside adobe settlement on the left in the distance, arches of buried bone, vermilion pennants. A sweep of negative space in the pale celadon and ivory sky, strong asymmetric flat ink silhouette and dry brush line, expressive old Japanese screen painting and modern fantasy illustration craft, restrained cinnabar, aubergine ink and washed sand. Lyrical, windswept, beautiful and unusually graphic. Crop-friendly. No lettering, panels, border, symbols, writing, logos or recognisable game characters.

For a river, city or shrine, specify a dominant brush movement and a small counterweight such as a boat or doorway. Keep the open paper. The direction of a creature within its painting and the position of that painting on the page are independent decisions.

## Specimen triptych

> A single wide lithographed scientific specimen plate, 3:1 aspect ratio, three independent fantasy minerals precisely aligned left to right in THREE equal-width columns. Left centre at one sixth: a squat triangular cluster of copper-red faceted crystals sharply leaning right, warm orange glow at core. Middle centre: aqua blue frost quartz with tall thin vertical blades, ice-blue facets. Right centre at five sixths: angular sulphur-gold branching fulgurite, forked like a lightning-struck twig, visually unlike quartz. All three floating upright on the SAME flat warm very light ivory paper background with small subtle ink shadows only, each centred within its own third, generous identical padding including top and bottom, no overlap. Vintage geological illustration with beautifully detailed ink cross-hatching, mineral pigments and gouache highlights. Very legible shapes even as small thumbnails. This is an unlabelled original field-guide object plate. Absolutely no letters, typography, labels, numbers, symbols, rules, frames, border, logos or people.
