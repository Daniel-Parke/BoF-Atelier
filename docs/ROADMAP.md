# What we're building together

The finished project should let someone replay the games they remember, consult an illustrated guide without losing their place, and choose exactly which changes to bring into a new campaign. **Original** preserves the game's rules, presentation and music. **Enhanced** will offer a tested selection of optional changes. **Personal** will let players choose and review their own combinations.

## The remaster

Every image, animation and music cue is within the long-term remit. A usable replacement needs more than a larger image or a new recording. A frame must line up with the next frame and with its surroundings. A battle track must return to the right field cue after victory and yield cleanly if the player disables it. III and IV have distinct visual styles and scores. Each proposed asset belongs to a named game and scene.

Packs should state the base revision, changes, dependencies, conflicts, save impact, permissions and fallback. Changes to rules, difficulty, artistic interpretation and new content should be individually identifiable. A change written into a save can survive disabling its pack. A persistent change needs a pre-change branch and a demonstrated recovery path.

## Useful first contributions

- **Play a route.** Verify a named exit, item location or last chance to return. Record the game, edition, starting point and what you observed.
- **Improve an illustration.** Offer an original diagram of a confusing junction with a matching text direction. Record its origin and any visual references.
- **Review an animation or cue.** Describe its start, loop and end in a named scene. That record can become a repeatable comparison before any replacement is created.
- **Test the reader.** Use keyboard navigation, a small screen or a screen reader. Give the page, browser, action and expected result.
