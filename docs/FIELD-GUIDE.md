# Inside the Field Guide

[Open the online or offline reader](../README.md#read-the-field-guide). Choose **III** or **IV**. **Walkthrough** follows the journey in order. **Field wiki** looks up a subject by name. Switch volumes to search the other game.

## Breath of Fire III

Start at **Dauna Mine** or go to **Cedar Woods and Rei’s house** for the next part of the opening journey. Each route page brings its steps together with relevant items, encounters and map notes. Use the directions if you want to find your own way between landmarks.

For a different way into the book, open **Dragon genes** in the Field wiki. Select **Flame** and **Frost** together. Their affinities cancel. The resulting neutral Dragon costs **10 AP** to transform. Change a gene to compare the result. The public reader supplies three illustrated specimens to try. The desktop edition can read further data from a recognised local disc.

## Breath of Fire IV

Open **Kurok, the Dam and Kyria Woods**. In **Kurok**, the upper fork leads to a red chest with the Deep Diver fishing lure. Return to the junction and follow the lower western branch to reach the Dam. Switch to **Dam** for the directions that apply there. The same stop can hold several areas with different routes.

For a character question, look up **Masters** in the Field wiki or search for **Rwolf**. The browser offers sample master records. The two games handle dragons and growth differently, so check which volume you have open.

## Keep your place

Set the spoiler boundary on the stop you have reached. Later names stay hidden in lists and searches until you choose to reveal them. Bookmark the page to find it again. Browser settings can prevent bookmarks from persisting, especially for local files. You can also keep the page address, including the part after `#`. For example, `#bof4/dam-kyria?area=Dam` opens the Dam directly.

The browser reads the selected page without opening a save. The desktop Player can suggest a walkthrough at a recognised point in a local campaign. If a visit's history is unknown, choose the page yourself.
