# How the walkthrough is checked

Use the English PlayStation version when checking a route in these books. A port, translation or different region may use a different name or rule. The sources below are community authors’ own walkthroughs and research.

GameFAQs marks DavidK5, Dalez and Billy_Lee as “Most Recommended” in these indexes. RealXaenith’s later guide also lists locations individually. These are useful ways to find different accounts of the same journey. A recommendation badge says nothing about a particular chest, drop rate or button.

Each of the 50 story stops links to sections by at least two authors. Open **Sources** on a walkthrough page to find their accounts. Some older text guides include search codes. For example, search Tricrokra’s walkthrough for `EP100000013` to find Beyd’s training or `EP200000011` for the Steel Beach hauling game. [Tricrokra’s walkthrough](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196817-breath-of-fire-iii/faqs/12039)

## When two guides differ

Identify the condition each author used. A character in different clothes, the other side of a gate, an earlier save or a different game revision can explain a difference. Prefer a repeatable in-game observation for directions. Keep the conflicting reference with the evidence until somebody resolves it.

- **Cedar Woods.** The bedside diary and the upstairs balcony are near one another. The route begins at the diary, goes downstairs and leaves through the front door. A Lab replay followed the bends, crossed the log and opened the Skill Ink chest.
- **The Dam.** One decision happens before the second engine starts. Save first if you want another attempt at its fishing-lure prize. A separate decision comes when returning the Sluice Key. The two warnings belong at their own steps.
- **Mt. Glaus.** Follow the blood to the skeleton for 200 zenny. Then retrace your steps and take the other passage to the upstream bank. The Lab route checked both Nue encounters and the river exit. [DavidK5, Mt. Glaus](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196817-breath-of-fire-iii/faqs/18685) · [Tricrokra, Mt. Glaus](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196817-breath-of-fire-iii/faqs/12039)
- **Kyria village.** The bard charges 10 zenny for the tadpole song. The dragon song costs 100. The Lab party tried both and found 400 zenny through the village pit. Kyria Woods has its own treasure and encounter list. [Dalez, I.J to I.K](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/9877) · [Billy Lee, Kyria](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/9833)
- **Kyria Woods.** The US game awarded one Apple and one Flame Punch on the same results screen. The party used no steal action. Navarchos also lists the Apple as a steal. Its appearance after one victory establishes a possible reward. The book uses the enemy table’s 3,600 HP. Dalez and RealXaenith give 3,500. The Lab run verified the rewards and the Hwa and Sever combo. It did not measure Maman’s starting HP. [Dalez, I.K](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/9877) · [RealXaenith, Kyria Woods](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/73540) · [navarchos, Maman](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/80701)
- **Passage to Synesta.** The Lab party collected the Water Bomb across the first stream and the Ammonia on the dry tunnel’s north spur. It left the 500-zenny chest closed and climbed into town. After the arrival conversation, it went back down the well, took the western spur off the flooded steps and jumped north to collect the money. The treasure list marks all three chests as available on a later visit. Another Ammonia sits on a shelf in Synesta Cellar. [Dalez, I.L](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/9877) · [Billy_Lee, Missing Princess](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/9833) · [mev1978, entries 039 to 042](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/80045)
- **Skills in the passage.** RealXaenith describes learning Shadowwalk from an Army Bat here. The enemy table gives it 8 HP and puts the skill below one quarter health, which leaves a window of exactly 1 HP. A Tadpole can demonstrate Snooze while it has at least half its HP. A Cadaver can demonstrate Feint. These are optional encounters, and Guard gives a chance to learn. [RealXaenith, Synesta Pathway](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/73540) · [navarchos, enemy behaviour and encounter areas 020 and 022](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/80701)
- **The children of Synesta.** Dalez counts seven children around town. The US game confirmed the six hiding places in Tricrokra’s list. Jahn gave the first clue, and the other child walking around the orphanage said they were sitting out. The party had to step down onto Nahma’s staircase to speak to her. Modo stood on the lower street visible from the bridge. Konoko was at the inn’s upstairs landing. [Dalez, I.M](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/9877) · [Tricrokra, IDXE006](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/47149) · [RealXaenith, Synesta](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/73540)
- **The cellar guard.** Walking into him produced a refusal. Using Talk brought up his thirst. Speaking to the water seller when she came close then opened the way. [Tricrokra, IDXC00H](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/47149) · [DEH Designs, Synesta](https://www.dehdesigns.com/bof_iv/pages/walkthrough.php)
- **Synesta Cellar.** The party collected Ammonia from the northern bookcase in the first side room, Protein from behind the bar, 80 zenny from the upstairs bookcase and Raptor Claws from the lower jail. Stepping on the far upper cell’s wooden cover dropped the party beside Chino. Walking around the jar was enough to reach him. Talking to him returned everyone to the orphanage. Roach appeared in the captured battles. The Mouse and ToxicFly entries use the published encounter table. [mev1978, treasures 042 to 045](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/80045) · [Billy_Lee, Synesta](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/9833) · [navarchos, area 024](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/80701)
- **Castle Fou-Lu.** Goo King carries an Apple to steal. The GooKingSword is a rare drop after the fight. The castle notes separate the two rewards. [Dalez, Castle Fou-Lu](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/9877) · [navarchos, Goo King](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/80701)
- **Master growth in IV.** Published tables disagree about Gyosil and Lyta. A local read of the US PlayStation game found no Defence adjustment for Gyosil. Lyta changes AP by +1, Defence by −1 and Wisdom by +1. The public book records individual stat adjustments relative to studying under no master. [DEH Designs’ master tables](https://www.dehdesigns.com/bof_iv/pages/secrets.php) · [Matt Hobbs’s PlayStation FAQ](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/10937)

## Before calling a page complete

Work through the [guide editing standard](FIELD-GUIDE-STYLE.md) against the cited chapter, then replay any decision that is disputed or costly to get wrong. Keep a source-only finding and a gameplay result separate. Describe the first visit and the return visit independently. Leave optional system details one link away. Link the authors’ own pages so readers can consult the original accounts.
