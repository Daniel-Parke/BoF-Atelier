Use the English PlayStation version when checking a route in these books. A port, translation or different region may use a different name or rule. The sources below are community authors’ own walkthroughs and research.

GameFAQs marks DavidK5, Dalez and Billy_Lee as “Most Recommended” in these indexes. RealXaenith’s later guide also lists locations individually. These are useful ways to find different accounts of the same journey. A recommendation badge says nothing about a particular chest, drop rate or button.

Each of the 50 story stops links to sections by at least two authors. Open **Sources** on a walkthrough page to find their accounts. Some older text guides include search codes. For example, search Tricrokra’s walkthrough for `EP100000013` to find Beyd’s training or `EP200000011` for the Steel Beach hauling game. ## When two guides differ

Identify the condition each author used. A character in different clothes, the other side of a gate, an earlier save or a different game revision can explain a difference. Prefer a repeatable in-game observation for directions. Keep the conflicting reference with the evidence until somebody resolves it.

- **Cedar Woods.** The bedside diary and the upstairs balcony are near one another. The route begins at the diary, goes downstairs and leaves through the front door. A Lab replay followed the bends, crossed the log and opened the Skill Ink chest.
- **The Dam.** One decision happens before the second engine starts. Save first if you want another attempt at its fishing-lure prize. A separate decision comes when returning the Sluice Key. The two warnings belong at their own steps.
- **Master growth in IV.** Published tables disagree about Gyosil and Lyta. A local read of the US PlayStation game found no Defence adjustment for Gyosil. Lyta changes AP by +1, Defence by −1 and Wisdom by +1. The public book records individual stat adjustments relative to studying under no master. [DEH Designs’ master tables](https://www.dehdesigns.com/bof_iv/pages/secrets.php) · [Matt Hobbs’s PlayStation FAQ](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/196818-breath-of-fire-iv/faqs/10937)

## Before calling a page complete

Work through the [guide editing standard](FIELD-GUIDE-STYLE.md) against the cited chapter, then replay any decision that is disputed or costly to get wrong. Keep a source-only finding and a gameplay result separate. Describe the first visit and the return visit independently. Leave optional system details one link away. Link the authors’ own pages so readers can consult the original accounts.
